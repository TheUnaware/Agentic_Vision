05 · Rear Hub

The brain stem.

  • ESP32-S3Sensor hub — syncs cameras, ToF & IMU, streams to the phone over Wi-Fi and plays back audio.
  • 6-axis IMUKnows which way your body faces, so “left” always means your left. Detects falls.
  • USB-C + chargerSingle-port charging with Li-ion protection & power-path.
  • 2 battery lobesLi-Po cells either side of the neck for balance and comfort all day.
06 · Uplink

Neckband → Phone

Four synchronised camera streams, four 64-zone ToF depth grids and IMU orientation flow to the Android app. The phone is the edge-AI computer — no cloud, no network, no privacy leaks.

07 · On-Phone Agentic Pipeline

From pixels to a plan.

  1. Continuous input4 cameras + ToF + IMU, frame-synced
  2. Object detection · YOLOv10NMS-free detector → classes + boxes
  3. Depth · MiDaS + ToFRelative depth scaled to metres
  4. Scene fusion{object, distance, direction, velocity}
  5. Trajectory · Kalman filterFuture positions 2–3 s ahead, time-to-collision
  6. Agentic reasoning · Phi-3 MiniYour goal + the scene → a decision
  7. Speech outputOn-device TTS → neckband earbuds
TensorRT · ONNX Runtime · INT4/INT8 quantised

10 · Why this has never existed

Navigation is a reasoning problem,
not a detection problem.

Every commercial assistive app stops at “there is a person”. Agentic Vision is the first wearable that closes the loop from perception to a spoken, goal-directed plan.

🧠

Agentic, goal-driven

A quantised Phi-3 Mini takes your goal and the live 3D scene and produces step-by-step guidance — “stop”, “slight left”, “continue straight” — not labels.

📏

Metric depth fusion

YOLOv10 + MiDaS fused into 3D bounding volumes, with VL53L5CX ToF anchoring relative depth to real metres: “Motorbike 1.2 m ahead, 30° right.”

⏱️

Predicts the future

A Kalman filter tracks every moving object and forecasts collision risk 2–3 seconds ahead — absent in all current commercial apps.

🌐

True 360° awareness

Four 120° cameras around the neck see behind and beside you. Phone-in-hand apps see only where the phone points.

🙌

Hands-free, ears-open

No phone to hold, no cane-mounted gadget, no sealed earbuds. A white cane or guide dog stays in the hand; ambient sound stays in the ears.

⚡

Edge-AI, offline

The full pipeline is optimised with TensorRT + ONNX Runtime and runs on the phone — low latency, works without network, and video never leaves the device.
